Kungjjak Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Kungjjak does not collect any personal information.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

  • Name, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number
  • Location, contacts, photos, files, call logs
  • Device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address
  • App usage, crash logs, diagnostics

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not identify you.

2. Network access

None. The Android app does not request the internet permission (INTERNET), so it does not communicate over the network. All fonts and graphics the games need are included in the app.

The web version served from the same address as this page (sya-apps.github.io) is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep access logs while a page is open, under GitHub's own policies. The game itself does not send anything, on the web either.

3. Information stored on your device

None. Scores and settings (including the language you pick) are kept in memory only and disappear when you close the app. The app does not use browser storage (localStorage) or cookies. When you open it again, it starts fresh.

4. Third parties, ads, and analytics

None. The app currently contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and it does not share or entrust any information to third parties.

5. Permissions

None. The app does not request camera, storage, location, contacts, or any other permission.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not designed for children under 13, and it does not collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Uninstalling the app removes it completely from your device.

8. Contact

Sodam Apps · sya.apps.dev@gmail.com

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated text and its effective date on this page. If a change is less favorable to users, we will announce it on this page at least 7 days before it takes effect.
